## Full Map w/ Roads
![Full Map](https://github.com/normal-platinum/MKW-Map/blob/main/Full_Map.png)

- Black lines are "Primary Roads", as in, used in races (GP, KT, VS, TT, etc.)
- Green lines are "Secondary Roads", not used in races
- Red lines are Railroad Lines
- Gray Dotted Lines are "Aerial Paths", of which many are one-way
- Blue Dotted Lines are "Water Paths"
- Yellow lines are "Highways", which always have 4 lanes and can be driven on by large vehicles
- Cyan lines are "Main Roads", which can be driven on by medium-sized vehicles
- Magenta lines are "Back Roads", which can only be driven on by small vehicles

### This project is incomplete as of the moment.
1) The road lines are very close to accurate, but I would say they are within maybe 3-5px in each direction of the actual location. This likely will remain the case until datamining becomes possible.
2) The map would benefit from being vectorized, however I am not skilled with vectorizing artwork programs.
3) Battle Tracks are a bit trickier to include (especially Salty Salty Speedway as it makes the map a lot harder to interpret).
4) In addition to the previous point, many free roam-only roads may be missing simply because traffic vehicles do not drive on them at all.
5) Height information is not directly implied in the map.
6) Secondary map features (like Koopa Beach 1) are not fully included yet.
7) "Daredevil Trails" (the ones that feature "Wayward Psychopaths", iykyk) are not yet included.
